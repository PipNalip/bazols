# Idea interview

Clarify the product without writing files or code.

1. Start with the user, problem, and desired outcome.
2. Ask one short question at a time. Prefer choices when the trade-off is concrete.
3. Resolve boundaries: first useful version, explicit exclusions, data ownership, accounts, external services, and actions with money or real people.
4. Turn decisions into 2-3 usage scenarios and observable acceptance criteria.
5. End with a compact brief: goal, user, scenarios, acceptance, out of scope, decisions, and open questions.
6. Ask whether to save that brief as a spec; do not save it without approval.

Do not lead with technology. Do not turn optional future ideas into MVP requirements.
